AMRITA VISHWAVIDYAPEEETHAM 

        

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham is a top-ranked, multi-campus private university in India, accredited with an A++ grade by NAAC.
Elite Rankings: Consistently ranked among India's top 10 universities by NIRF (8th in 2025) and placed among the top 50 globally in the Impact Rankings 2025.
Expansive Network: Operates across 10 campuses in six states, including Coimbatore (headquarters), Bengaluru, Kochi, and Faridabad.
Diverse Programs: Offers over 300 undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in fields like Engineering, Medicine, Management, and Ayurveda.
Research Focus: A research-intensive institution with over 150 patents granted and partnerships with nearly 290 funded projects.
Strong Placements: Maintains a high placement rate, with a 92% success rate in 2024 and average packages around ₹9.08 LPA for engineering graduates.
