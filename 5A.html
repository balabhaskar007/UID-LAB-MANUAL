Spotify

Spotify is a popular digital music streaming service that allows users to listen to millions of songs, podcasts, and audio content online. Founded in 2006, it lets users create playlists, discover new music, and follow their favorite artists anywhere. 🎧🎶

Netflix

Netflix is a popular streaming platform where users can watch movies, TV shows, and documentaries online. It offers a wide range of content in different languages and genres. People can watch Netflix on devices like smartphones, computers, and smart TVs through a monthly subscription. 🎬📺

Pintrest

Pinterest is a popular social media platform where users can discover and save ideas through images and videos. People use Pinterest to find inspiration for topics like fashion, recipes, home decoration, travel, and DIY projects. Users can create boards to organize their favorite “pins” and share them with others. The platform helps people explore creative ideas and plan projects visually. 📌📷
