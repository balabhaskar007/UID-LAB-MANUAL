INDIAN MUSIC

Apple music

Spotify is a popular digital music streaming service that allows users to listen to millions of songs, podcasts, and audio content online. Founded in 2006 in Sweden, it offers both free (ad-supported) and premium subscription plans. Spotify lets users create playlists, discover new music through recommendations, and follow their favorite artists. The platform is available on smartphones, computers, smart TVs, and other devices, making it easy for people around the world to enjoy music anytime and anywhere. 🎧🎶

Gaana

Gaana is an Indian music streaming platform that allows users to listen to millions of songs, podcasts, and radio shows online. Launched in 2010 by Times Internet, Gaana offers music in many languages including Hindi, Punjabi, Tamil, and Telugu. Users can stream songs for free with ads or subscribe to Gaana Plus for an ad-free experience and offline downloads. The app is widely used in India for discovering new music, creating playlists, and enjoying popular Bollywood and regional songs. 🎧🎵

YouTube Music

JioSaavn is a popular Indian music streaming platform that offers a large collection of songs, podcasts, and radio shows. It was formed after the merger of Saavn and Reliance Jio’s music service in 2018. JioSaavn provides music in multiple languages such as Hindi, English, Punjabi, Tamil, and Telugu. Users can listen to songs for free with ads or subscribe to JioSaavn Pro for ad-free listening, higher audio quality, and offline downloads. 🎧🎶
