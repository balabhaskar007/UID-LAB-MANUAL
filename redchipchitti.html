DIGITAL PLATFORMS

Spotify

Spotify is a popular digital music streaming service that allows users to listen to millions of songs, podcasts, and audio content online. Founded in 2006, it lets users create playlists, discover new music, and follow their favorite artists anywhere. 🎧🎶

Gaana

Gaana is an Indian music streaming platform that allows users to listen to millions of songs online. Launched in 2010, Gaana offers music in many languages including Hindi, Punjabi, Tamil, and Telugu. The app is widely used for discovering Bollywood hits. 🎧🎵

JioSaavn

JioSaavn is a popular Indian music streaming platform that offers a large collection of songs, podcasts, and radio shows. Formed after the merger of Saavn and Reliance Jio’s music service, it provides ad-free listening and high audio quality for subscribers. 🎧🎶
